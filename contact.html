Frequently Asked Questions

What types of projects do you work on?

I specialize in web applications, mobile apps, and UI/UX design projects. I work with startups, established companies, and individual clients on projects ranging from MVPs to enterprise-level applications.

What is your typical project timeline?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A typical website takes 2-4 weeks, while more complex web applications can take 2-6 months. I'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

How do you charge for your services?

I offer both project-based and hourly pricing, depending on the project requirements. After understanding your needs, I'll provide a detailed proposal with transparent pricing. I also offer flexible payment plans for larger projects.

Do you provide ongoing support and maintenance?

Yes! I offer ongoing support and maintenance packages to ensure your project continues to run smoothly after launch. This includes bug fixes, updates, security patches, and feature enhancements.

What is your availability for new projects?

I'm currently accepting new projects. My availability varies based on current commitments, but I typically can start new projects within 1-2 weeks. Contact me to discuss your project timeline.
